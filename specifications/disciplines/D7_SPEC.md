## 3. Conceptual Candidate Controls (`PENDING_APPROVAL`)

1. **Protected, versioned reflow recipes** (access control and revision locking on SMT reflow ovens).
2. **Independent validation of critical changes** (mandatory thermal profile verification and quality sign-off prior to production release).
3. **Updated retest work instructions** (prohibiting unconditional release on retest PASS without root-cause disposition of the initial failure).
4. **Electronic holds** (automated MES/tester lock preventing movement of initial FAIL units until authorized disposition).
5. **Training, audits, control ownership, and frequency** (operator/technician training, layered process audits, designated owners, and defined review frequencies).
